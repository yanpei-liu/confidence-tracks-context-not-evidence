\begin{frame}[t]{Agenda}
  \begin{enumerate}\setlength\itemsep{0.6em}
    \item \textbf{Setup and expectation.}\\
      {\footnotesize\color{inkmid} Can model ``confidence'' serve as a proxy for metamemory?}
    \item \textbf{The result: confidence is evidence-blind.}\\
      {\footnotesize\color{inkmid} At every \dt\ where the no-evidence floor was measured, accuracy responds and confidence does not.}
    \item \textbf{What confidence actually tracks.}\\
      {\footnotesize\color{inkmid} The context-volume dose--response: a monotone on one measure, flat on the other.}
    \item \textbf{Off-letter mass: an implicit abstention signal.}\\
      {\footnotesize\color{inkmid} When evidence is gone, the model sometimes stops wanting to pick a letter.}
    \item \textbf{AUROC degradation: a genuinely new phenomenon.}\\
      {\footnotesize\color{inkmid} The confidence \emph{ranking} decays under a bounded cache, where it was flat before.}
  \end{enumerate}
