\begin{frame}[t]{Decisions I would like from you}
  \begin{enumerate}\setlength\itemsep{0.7em}
    \item[\textbf{1.}] \textbf{Retire the original hypothesis?} It has now failed in both regimes, the second of which was the setting it most deserved. My recommendation is to retire it and lead with the dissociation and its rotation.
    \item[\textbf{2.}] \textbf{Which follow-up first?} My order is: timestamps-off on \Eone, then recalibration on \texttt{live\_tokens}. The first is the cheapest thing that could change the story; the second is the corrected version of what we promised.
    \item[\textbf{3.}] \textbf{How hard to position against EgoSAT?} They hold the ``confidence under streaming'' flag; we hold the decomposition, the within-backbone comparison, the time scale and the statistics. I would cite them as convergent evidence in the introduction rather than fight for the framing.
    \item[\textbf{4.}] \textbf{Is the abstention channel worth a section?} It is free, it is a measured effect where we currently report a null, and it is the only genuinely metacognitive signal in either experiment --- but it is a 1.4\,\% tail and it needs the top-$k$ dump to say what those tokens are.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{frame}
